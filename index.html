What is APValues?

APValues is, in essence, an Archipelago settings quiz that attempts to assign percentages for ten different opinions about how a multiworld should be run. You will be presented by a statement, and then you will answer with your opinion on the statement, from Strongly Agree to Strongly Disagree, with each answer slightly affecting your scores. At the end of the quiz, your answers will be compared to the maximum possible for each value, thus giving you a percentage. Answer honestly!

There are questions in the test.

What are the ten values?

There are five independent axes - Hint, DeathLink, Deadline, Check Count, and Bonus Link - and each has two opposing values assigned to them. They are:

FREE HINTS

Those with higher Free Hints scores believe hints should be cheap or free and shared with everyone who's stuck. They tend to support generous hint point rates, community trackers, and at high values, hints for all from the start.

HINT

HINT ECONOMY

Those with higher Hint Economy scores believe hints should be earned and scarce. They tend to support steep hint costs, checks-per-hint requirements, and at high values, no hints at all.

DEATHLINK BANNED

Those with higher DeathLink Banned scores think no one in the multiworld should be allowed to use it. They often prefer each game to stand entirely on its own, valuing independence, and at high values, outright forbid it from being enabled.

DEATHLINK

DEATHLINK MANDATORY

Those with higher DeathLink Mandatory scores think everyone in the multiworld should be required to play with it on. They often believe in shared stakes and a connected multiworld, and at high values, would force it for every player.

FREEPLAY

Those with higher Freeplay scores believe in playing at your own pace. They tend to oppose hard cutoffs for YAML submission or race clocks, valuing relaxed, casual multiworlds above all else.

DEADLINE

DEADLINE

Those with higher Deadline scores believe in firm, enforced schedules. They tend to support strict YAML submission cutoffs, race-style time limits, and at high values, no exceptions for anyone.

MINIMAL

Those with higher Minimal scores believe in small, lean check counts with little or no filler. Though not always, they usually prefer close-to-vanilla games and support keeping multiworlds short and tight.

CHECK COUNT

MAXIMAL

Those with higher Maximal scores believe in big, expansive check counts, filler and all. Though not always, they usually favor checksanity-style settings and support long, content-packed multiworlds.

NO EXTRAS

Those with higher No Extras scores think bonus link features like EnergyLink or RingLink are unnecessary distractions. They tend to prefer a clean, focused multiworld and at high values, would leave every bonus feature off.

BONUS LINK

ALL EXTRAS

Those with higher All Extras scores love turning on every bonus link feature available. They tend to embrace EnergyLink, RingLink, and whatever new shared mechanic comes along, and at high values, want them enabled by default.
